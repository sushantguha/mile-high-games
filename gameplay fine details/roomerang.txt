Here's the full turn-by-turn of Roomerang (latest version in The Jackbox Party Pack 9), tracking the main screen and each player's phone separately. Sourced exclusively from jackboxgames.fandom.com and jackbox.wiki.

Setup and lobby
The host launches Roomerang on the shared/main screen. A four-letter room code appears; players join on phones with the code and a display name. Player count: 4–9. Audience can answer separate polls when Audience mode is on. Session length is roughly 30 minutes.

Character creation (before Round 1)
**Main screen:** Lobby fills with "roomie" avatars as players join.
**Player phones:** Confirm your display name. Choose a character detail from three preset roles or type a custom one (e.g. "Likes Smoothies"). Pick one of twelve monochromatic "roomie" avatars. Your name, avatar, and a matching voice sound appear in the lobby.

Game start
**Main screen:** Host Rue Meringue (voiced by Dezi Bing) explains the rules, then introduces each roomie and their role in a reality-TV-style house.
**Player phones:** Each player starts with **5 points** in a private bank. No one sees anyone else's score until after the final round — only your own total is visible on your device throughout play.

Five rounds — shared loop (Rounds 1–4)
Each of the first four rounds follows the same challenge → vote → elimination → Roomerang cycle, with round-specific prompt types:

| Round | Name | Prompt type |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Introductions | Generic self-intro prompt |
| 2 | Connections | Prompt pairs you with a randomly selected other player |
| 3 | Quickie | Generic prompt, but identities stay hidden until after voting |
| 4 | Firestarter | Pick another player and blame them for something |

Challenge phase
**Main screen:** Rue presents the round prompt. In Round 3 (Quickie), answers appear anonymously on-screen.
**Player phones:** Answer the prompt in character (text entry). Audience members answer a separate poll if Audience is enabled.

Reveal and vote phase
**Main screen:** Submitted answers are revealed one by one. Vote tallies appear after voting closes.
**Player phones:** After all answers are shown, you (and the audience) vote for the best answer. Each vote a player receives = **1 point** added to their private bank. The challenge winner earns a special advantage (see below).

Challenge winner advantages (varies by round)
- Safety from elimination
- Safety + give another player safety
- Safety + audience picks who to eliminate (Audience mode only)
- Safety + mentioned player gets safety (Rounds 2 and 4)
- Swap points with another player (Rounds 3 and 4)
- Winner chooses who to eliminate (their vote counts double)
- Winner chooses who to eliminate among those who voted for the winning answer (vote doubled)
- Winner's vote counts as two
- Winner can cancel another player's vote

Elimination phase
**Main screen:** Players (except the winner with safety) vote on who should leave the house and why. Rue reveals the eliminated player by pulling a card from an envelope — the reveal animation gets slightly faster each round. The eliminated player's door slams shut after parting words.
**Player phones:** Vote for who to eliminate and type a reason. The eliminated player gets a few seconds for goodbye text or speech. Throughout the game, an **inbox** on the bottom-left of the controller shows who tried to eliminate you or who won challenges — useful for deciding your next elimination vote.

Roomerang (after elimination)
The eliminated player immediately returns with:
- A new accessory (hat, glasses, mustache, etc.)
- A new name (family-friendly off: first vowel swapped — e.g. BROOKE → BReOKE; family-friendly on: affix added to existing name)
- A new character description (e.g. "Has Never Worn Shoes")

Round 5 — Finale
**Main screen:** Rue asks for a final prompt, usually involving a lesson, moral, or learning experience.
**Player phones:** Submit your answer. Then vote **who should win** — points doubled per vote received. Then vote **who should NOT win** — each vote deducts a point from that player. Final scores are revealed in ascending order until the top two appear side by side.

End of game and tie-breakers
The player with the most points wins and gives a victory speech before the door closes on them. Tie-break order:
1. Most "who should win" votes
2. Fewest "who shouldn't win" votes
3. Audience pick (if still tied)
4. Random selection

After credits, the VIP can replay with the same players and identities (scores reset by default; November Party Pack 9 update added option to keep scores).

To be straight about the limits: round types, scoring privacy, elimination flow, Roomerang name/role changes, and advantage list are well-documented. Exact timer seconds per writing/voting phase are not published on the wikis.
