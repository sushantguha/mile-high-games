Here's the full turn-by-turn of Fakin' It All Night Long (stylized Fakin' It! All Night Long; latest version in The Jackbox Naughty Pack), tracking the main screen and each player's phone separately. Sourced exclusively from jackboxgames.fandom.com and jackbox.wiki.

Setup and lobby
The host launches Fakin' It All Night Long on the shared/main screen. A four-letter room code appears; players join on phones with the code and a display name. Player count: 3–8 (+ Audience). Cookie Masterson (voiced by Tom Gottlieb) hosts. Choose from ten swimwear avatars (returning colors Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Purple plus Teal, Pink Dog, Robot, Gray Cat/Mayonnaise). Pick **Same Room** or **Remote Play** from the main menu. Session length is roughly 20–30 minutes. Mature content — family-friendly filter available via post-launch update.

Game structure — four rounds
Rounds 1–3: players vote a category from a pool of five. Round 4 is always **Text You Up** (no category vote).

Each category round — three tasks
One random player is the **Faker** each round (except Text You Up twist below). Non-Fakers get a secret task; the Faker is told they are the Faker and must blend in. After each task (except Thumb Shot and Text You Up — see below), players vote for who they think is the Faker. **Majority vote** catches the Faker — not unanimous (e.g. 5 of 8). First-time players: Cookie auto-picks **Hands of Truth** for Round 1.

Categories (Rounds 1–3)

**Hands of Truth** (returning)
- **Main screen:** Prompt displayed; avatars raise hands.
- **Player phones:** Prompt or "blend in" text if Faker. Two buttons: raise hand / don't. Remote: character raises hand on-screen.

**You Gotta Point** (returning)
- **Player phones:** Prompt or blend-in text. Tap a player name (including yourself) who best fits.

**Finger Blast** (was "Number Pressure")
- **Player phones:** Prompt or blend-in text. Buttons for 0–5 fingers raised. Small bird button bottom-right flips the bird (remote play). Vote after each of three tasks.

**Emojional Damage** (was "Face Value")
- **Player phones:** Prompt or blend-in text. Searchable emoji board by category.

**Thumb Shot** (new)
- **Player phones:** Three quick prompts in a row with two answers each (e.g. "SEXY or NOT SEXY"). Thumbs-up = first option, thumbs-down = second. **One vote total** after all three prompts.

Voting after tasks
- **Main screen:** Vote tallies; Faker revealed if majority agrees.
- **Player phones:** Tap the player you suspect. Can vote after tasks 1, 2, and 3 in most categories.

Round 4 — Text You Up (always final)
- **Player phones:** Three text prompts answered one by one. **The Faker does not know they are the Faker** until all answers are submitted — they receive vaguer prompts. After each question, players may informally suspect someone, but official voting happens only after all three answers are shown together.
- **Main screen:** All three Q&A sets display; one final Faker vote.

Scoring
| Bonus | Points |
|-------|--------|
| Sleuth Bonus (correct Faker pick, per task) | 100 → 125 → 150 (escalating per correct guess in round) |
| Caught Bonus (majority catches Faker, task 1) | 375 to all correct voters |
| Caught Bonus (task 2) | 250 first-time correct; 275 second-time |
| Caught Bonus (task 3) | 105 / 130 / 155 (Sleuth + 5) |
| Faker Bonus (avoid majority, per task) | 100 → 125 → 150 |
| Thumb Shot Faker escape | 150 Faker Bonus |
| Thumb Shot caught | 150 Caught Bonus |
| Thumb Shot sleuth when Faker escaped | 100 Sleuth Bonus |

End of game
Highest points wins. **Best Faker** and **Best Sleuth** honorable mentions also display.

To be straight about the limits: Remote Play inputs, majority-not-unanimous rule, Text You Up hidden-Faker twist, category behaviors, and scoring table are documented on Fandom and jackbox.wiki. Same Room physical gestures are less detailed on wiki — Remote Play is the fully documented path.
