Here's the full turn-by-turn of Monster Seeking Monster (the version in The Jackbox Party Pack 4 — meta: 3–7 players plus audience, ~15 minutes, Roleplay / Hidden Identity, not family-friendly), tracking the main screen and each player's phone separately. Felicia Shadowlurker hosts (voiced by Erica Elam). Sourced from jackboxgames.fandom.com and jackbox.wiki only. jackbox.wiki gameplay page is under construction — most turn detail is from Fandom.

Setup and lobby
The host launches Monster Seeking Monster on the shared/main screen. A four-letter room code appears; players join on phones. Three to seven players plus audience. Each player receives a secret Monster Power on their phone — bonuses may help the holder, hurt rivals, or both (Fandom). Human disguises mask monsters until reveal. If four or fewer players with audience off, a Robot CPU may fill the room (Fandom Robot section).

Game structure — six "nights"
Each night has two phases: (1) messaging, like a dating app, then (2) date requests. Scoring currency is hearts, not points. Most hearts at game end wins — unless the Zombie apocalypse condition triggers (see below).

A single night, step by step
Messaging phase
Main screen: Dating-app framing; from night two onward, one of 99 possible secret words is displayed on screen (Fandom Secret Words list — e.g. Totally, Creepy, Jackbox, Moist, etc.). If any message contains the active secret word, the screen flashes with lightning and a sound effect (Fandom).
Phone: Each player may send up to four messages per night to other players (Fandom). Audience-as-eighth-player: when audience joins, they occupy a collective globe-disguise slot. Audience gets mad-lib prompts sent via main screen or as replies; audience votes on fill-ins; majority answer is used. Only players who messaged the Audience can be dated by them (Fandom).

Date-request phase
Main screen: Date selection results and match animations.
Phone: Each player chooses one other player to ask on a date (Fandom).
Outcomes (Fandom):
- Mutual pick → successful date; both gain a heart.
- One-sided pick → rejection; rejected player loses nothing.
- No pick → penalty: lose a heart.
Monster powers may add or steal hearts when their conditions trigger (listed below).

Monster reveals (progressive)
After night 2: the current first-place player's monster power is revealed to everyone (Fandom).
Each following night: the next highest-ranked still-unrevealed player is revealed (Fandom). Powers should stay secret until reveal — except Vengeful Ghost is sometimes acknowledged as benefiting others too (Fandom tips still recommend secrecy).

Monster powers (end-of-game or conditional hearts — Fandom)
- Mummy: dates curse partners; curse spreads through their dates; at end steals half a heart from each cursed player unless everyone is cursed (then no steal). Marked with ankh.
- Vampire: dates turn others vampire (does not overwrite original power); original Vampire gets +½ bonus heart per vampire at end. Marked with bat.
- Vengeful Ghost: starts with +1 heart; if rejected when asking someone out, that player loses a heart.
- Serial Killer: steals two hearts from partners on the second night they date the same person.
- Leprechaun: unrevealed dates grant partners two fake hearts; fake hearts vanish on reveal; Leprechaun gains +1 heart per player who drops in rankings when revealed.
- Zombie: dates infect others; infections spread next night; if everyone is infected at end, only original Zombie wins regardless of hearts. Marked with zombie hand.
- Monster Hunter: +1 bonus heart per date with the secretly assigned target monster type, applied at end when hunt target is revealed.
- Glob: every time three different players are successfully dated, heart count doubles.
- Two-Faced Creep: +1 heart per rejection they issue; lose those bonus hearts if they fail to get a date themselves that night.
- Witch: collects one hair per new partner dated; on reveal, +1 heart per hair.
- Body Swapper: on full-moon nights, swaps all hearts with dated partner.
- Ventriloquist Puppet: +1 heart when dating someone who was rejected the previous night.
- Werewolf: on full-moon nights, +1 heart for dating; partners become werewolves (spread only on full moons); −½ heart when rejected on full moon. Marked with wolf icon.
- Invisible Person: starts with +2 hearts; appears in last place on scoreboard until final reveal.
- Mother: +1 heart whenever their randomly assigned child fails to get a date. Reveal text: "[PLAYER1] is [PLAYER2]'s Mother."
- Robot (CPU): loses one heart each night automatically; gains +1 heart when messaged and asked out even if rejected; if last at end, becomes evil and everyone loses.

Full moon
Some powers reference full-moon nights (Body Swapper, Werewolf — Fandom tips section). Exact night schedule is not published in the wikis.

End of game
Main screen: Monster identities, infection/curse markers, final heart counts, winner declared. Zombie universal-infection overrides normal victory (Fandom). Game length roughly 20–25 minutes (Fandom) vs 15 minutes (jackbox.wiki meta).
