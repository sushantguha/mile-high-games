Here's the full turn-by-turn of Doominate (stylized DOOMINATE; latest version in The Jackbox Party Pack 11), tracking the main screen and each player's phone separately. Sourced exclusively from jackboxgames.fandom.com and jackbox.wiki.

Setup and lobby
The host launches Doominate on the shared/main screen. A four-letter room code appears; players join on phones with the code and a display name. Player count: 3–8 (+ Audience). Jim Reaper (voiced by Brandon Paul Eells) hosts. Pick one of eight colored flame avatars. Session length is roughly 15–20 minutes.

Game premise
Head-to-head joke writing: take something nice and "ruin" it with a follow-up that makes opponents laugh. Round 3 flips the script — "un-ruin" previously ruined scenarios. Most points wins.

Round 1 — ruin two prompts
**Writing phase**
- **Main screen:** Jim introduces the round; angel imagery accompanies prompts.
- **Player phones:** Receive two subjects — pick one to ruin yourself; the game assigns the other. Complete each scenario with text that ruins the pleasant setup (e.g. "A Big Bowl of Spaghetti" → "Made from the finest human hair"). Tap **"Ruin it For Me"** for a game-generated answer if stuck.

**Voting phase**
- **Main screen:** One prompt appears with two players' answers shown Quiplash-style (two angels motif). Vote results display.
- **Player phones:** Vote for the answer that best ruined the scenario. The two featured players earn a **percentage of points** based on vote share. If **all** players and audience vote for the same answer, the winner gets a **"Doomination."**

**Between rounds:** Scoreboard on main screen.

Round 2 — ruin someone else's favorite thing (double points)
**Favorite-things phase**
- **Player phones:** Answer two prompts about your favorite thing in a category (TV show, movie, etc.). **"Gimme One"** submits a random subject for you.

**Ruin phase**
- **Player phones:** Choose another player's submitted favorite and write a ruination of it. Also ruin a second assigned prompt.

**Voting phase**
- Same head-to-head voting as Round 1, but points are **doubled**.
- **Judgement Vote bonus:** If the player whose favorite was ruined votes for your ruination, you earn extra credit.

Round 3 — un-ruin (triple points)
**Player phones:** Pick two pairings from previously ruined scenarios across Rounds 1–2. Each pairing shows the original prompt, the ruining answer, and your new "un-ruin" text.

**Voting phase**
- Same voting structure; points **tripled**.
- Unanimous all-player-plus-audience vote for one answer awards **"Redoomption"** (parallel to Round 1's Doomination).

End of game
All round scores tally; highest total wins. Jim Reaper presides over final standings.

To be straight about the limits: three-round structure, Ruin/Gimme One buttons, percentage-based voting points, double/triple multipliers, Doomination/Redoomption, and Judgement Vote are documented. Exact percentage tables per player count are not published on the wikis.
