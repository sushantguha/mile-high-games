Here's the full turn-by-turn of Cookie Haus (stylized COOKIE Haus; latest version in The Jackbox Party Pack 11), tracking the main screen and each player's phone separately. Sourced exclusively from jackboxgames.fandom.com and jackbox.wiki.

Setup and lobby
The host launches Cookie Haus on the shared/main screen. A four-letter room code appears; players join on phones with the code and a display name. Player count: 3–8 (+ Audience). Moonique (voiced by Olivia Nielsen) runs the bakery. Everyone decorates a round cookie on-device to set their profile picture. Session length is roughly 20 minutes.

Four rounds — structure overview
| Round | Task |
|-------|------|
| 1 | Customer prompt → pick shape → decorate → name cookie → vote |
| 2 | New customer prompt → same decorate/name/vote flow |
| 3 | Fill-in-the-blank prompt → name must complete the blank → vote |
| 4 | Returning Round 1 customer asks you to modify your top-scoring cookie from Round 1 |

Rounds 1 and 2 — standard bake
**Prompt phase**
- **Main screen:** A customer walks in (kangaroo, claw-themed fashionista, etc.) and requests cookies matching a topic (e.g. "things found in your dreams").
- **Player phones:** Select a cookie shape. Top two shapes are labelled **"challenge cookies."** Decorate with icing (color, brush size), sprinkles, and cookie base color. Name the finished cookie.

**Voting phase**
- **Main screen:** Cookies pair off head-to-head (three-way matchups possible in 5- or 7-player games). Vote tallies and bonus awards display.
- **Player phones:** Vote for your favorite in each matchup. Optionally give a **like/kudos** to the other cookie.

Bonuses after each matchup (ascending value)
- **Good Sport Bonus — 10 pts** per kudos you give another player (+50 Kudos Bonus to them)
- **Speedbaker Bonus — 25 pts** if submitted in ≤90 seconds; **Super-Speedbaker — 50 pts** (double) if ≤60 seconds
- **Like Bonus — 25 pts** per like received
- **Challenge Shape Bonus — 50 pts** for picking a challenge cookie shape
- **Baker's Duo Bonus — 50 pts** if your cookie appears in a three-way matchup (5- or 7-player only)
- **Weekly Special Bonus — 100 pts** for using the seasonal weekly sprinkle (e.g. Tasteful Stretch Denim Week)
- **Most Votes Bonus — 100 pts** for winning the matchup
- **Audience Bonus — 100 pts** for most audience votes
- **U-NOM-imous Bonus — 250 pts** if every voting player picks your cookie

Round 3 — fill-in-the-blank
Same decorate flow, but the customer prompt has a blank your cookie **name** must fill in. Voting and bonuses work the same.

Round 4 — modify your best work
A returning customer from Round 1 asks players to skew prior cookies a certain way ("more dangerous," "more normal," etc.).
- **Player phones:** See your **three top-scoring cookies from Round 1** and re-ice one to fit the new prompt. The original cookie appears beside your updated design (post-update feature).
- Voting and bonuses proceed as before.

End of game
Final scores tally; top 3 winners revealed. Moonique quips on unanimous votes (e.g. "Total cookie knockout!"). Customer reactions scale with points earned.
