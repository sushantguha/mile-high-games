## Additional Notes
- **Pack 12**: Announced (May 2026) for release Fall 2026. Five brand-new games. Specific titles, player counts, and types TBA. Expected to follow the standard 5-game pack format with audience support.
- **Standalones** (not part of numbered packs):
  - Drawful 2 (2016): 3-8 players, Drawing, Audience: Yes, Family Friendly: Yes.
  - The Jackbox Survey Scramble (2024): Survey-based modes (HILO, BOUNCE, etc.). Uses real player-submitted one-word answers. Variable player counts (typically small groups + audience).
- **The Jackbox Megapicker**: Free Steam-only launcher / library organizer for your owned Jackbox titles. Not a game.
- **Game Type / Secondary Type**: Loose categories to help with filtering and recommendations (Drawing, Trivia, Fill-In-The-Blank / Writing, Hidden Identity / Roleplay, Teamwork, Audio, Music, Other, etc.).
- Some games (e.g. Bracketeering up to 16, certain audience-heavy titles) have higher max active or total participants.
- For the absolute latest or exact current specs, check the official Jackbox Games website or in-game settings, as patches can tweak player counts or add features.
