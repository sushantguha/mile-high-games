HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Hypnotorious is a Jackbox-style hidden identity game for 4-8 players.
Typical session length: 20 minutes.

GAME FLOW:
1. Join the room on your phone with the room code shown on the host screen.
2. The host phone displays prompts, drawings, votes, and scores for everyone to see.
3. Follow on-screen instructions each round — write, draw, vote, or choose on your device.
4. Earn points by being funny, convincing, correct, or creative depending on the round.
5. Player with the most points at the end wins.

This recreation captures the core hidden identity mechanics for road-trip play on mobile devices.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The host phone is the 'TV' — it shows room code, game state, prompts, submissions, and winners.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Each player uses their own phone to input answers, drawings, and votes privately.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Varies; typical Jackbox session runs about 20 minutes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Points awarded per round based on votes, correctness, or survival depending on game type.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Keep the host phone visible to everyone. Make sure all players are on the same WiFi/hotspot.
