────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The ultimate bluffing trivia game — write convincing lies for real trivia.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A bizarre but true fact is shown with a blank. Everyone fills in the missing answer.
All submissions (including the truth) are shuffled and displayed.
Vote for what you think is the REAL answer. Fool others to steal their votes.
Includes Fibbage Fan Favorites and Fibbage Enough About You modes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Shows the prompt, collects lies, reveals truth and scores.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Type a convincing lie, then vote for the real answer.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Typically 9 rounds.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Points for votes on your lie + points for finding the truth.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Match the tone of real answers. Obscure facts make the best lies.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
