============================================================
QUIPLASH 3
============================================================

Pack:          Jackbox Party Pack 7
Players:       3 - 8
Length:        15 minutes
Game Type:     Fill-In-The-Blank
Family Safe:   Yes
Audience Mode: Yes

IMAGE: images/quiplash-3.svg

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Write funny answers to prompts and vote on the best.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Each round gives an open-ended prompt (e.g., 'A terrible name for a coffee shop').
Everyone writes a funny response. Responses are paired head-to-head on the host screen.
All players and audience vote on the funnier answer. Winner gets points.
Includes Quiplash Kidding (kid-friendly) and Thriplash (three answers) modes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Displays prompts, head-to-head matchups, and vote results.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Write witty answers and vote between two choices.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Two writing rounds plus a final round.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Votes earn points; audience votes count too.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Specific beats generic. Puns and surprise twists win votes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
