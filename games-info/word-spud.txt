============================================================
WORD SPUD
============================================================

Pack:          Jackbox Party Pack 1
Players:       2 - 8
Length:        10 minutes
Game Type:     Writing
Family Safe:   No
Audience Mode: No

IMAGE: images/word-spud.svg

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Collaborative word association chain with secret constraints.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Players take turns adding a word related to the previous word.
Each player secretly receives a constraint (e.g., must include a certain letter, can't use a category).
Others vote on whether the word satisfies the hidden rule.
Fool the group without breaking your constraint.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Shows the word chain, reveals constraints after voting.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Submit one word per turn; vote on others' submissions.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Multiple chains across the session.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Points for fooling players and satisfying your constraint.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Pick words that seem natural but sneakily meet your secret rule.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TECHNICAL NOTES (THIS APP)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Join via room code; one phone hosts the shared screen
