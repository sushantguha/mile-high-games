============================================================
DRAWFUL ANIMATE
============================================================

Pack:          Jackbox Party Pack 8
Players:       3 - 10
Length:        15 minutes
Game Type:     Drawing
Family Safe:   Yes
Audience Mode: Yes

IMAGE: images/drawful-animate.svg

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Draw silly prompts, guess titles, then animate your favorite drawings.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Everyone gets a weird drawing prompt. Draw it on your phone.
Others type what they think the drawing is called (fake titles).
Everyone votes on the real title among fakes. Then animate a drawing frame-by-frame.
Points for fooling others and guessing correctly.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Shows drawings, title guesses, vote results, and animations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Draw, submit fake titles, vote, and create short animations.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Drawing rounds plus an animation finale.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Points for votes on fake titles and correct guesses.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Simple clear drawings work best on small phone screens.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TECHNICAL NOTES (THIS APP)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Game ID: drawful-animate
- Implemented in the Mile High Games React app
- Join via room code; one phone hosts the shared screen
- Works on mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Android Chrome)
- Requires all devices on the same local network / hotspot
