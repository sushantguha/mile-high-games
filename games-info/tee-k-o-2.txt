============================================================
TEE K.O. 2
============================================================

Pack:          Jackbox Party Pack 10
Players:       3 - 8
Length:        20 minutes
Game Type:     Drawing
Family Safe:   Yes
Audience Mode: Yes

IMAGE: images/tee-k-o-2.svg

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Draw t-shirt designs, battle in a bracket, and build a winning wardrobe.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOW TO PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Draw slogans and images for t-shirt prompts on your phone.
Designs are paired in a tournament bracket on the host screen.
Vote on which shirt you'd actually wear. Winners advance until one champion remains.
Collect shirts you voted for into your wardrobe.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HOST SCREEN (TV PHONE)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Runs the bracket tournament and displays designs.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAYER PHONES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Draw shirt designs and vote in head-to-head matchups.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ROUNDS & STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Drawing phase then elimination bracket.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bracket advancement; most wins takes the crown.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TIPS FOR ROAD TRIP PLAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bold simple designs read better on a phone host screen.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TECHNICAL NOTES (THIS APP)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Join via room code; one phone hosts the shared screen
